Off the Apps · A singles event

Dating + Dancing

A different approach to dating.

We're teaming up with Simple Love Counseling and Hobcaw Brewing for a different kind of singles event. Attendance is by application only: a marriage and family therapist reviews every application and curates the group so everyone in the room has something in common. Once you're accepted, you'll receive details on how to reserve your spot.

We'll bring the dancing. Our instructors lead an easy, beginner-friendly lesson where partners rotate throughout, so you'll have a chance to meet everyone there. No experience needed. Just show up, grab a drink, and let the dance floor do the introductions.
