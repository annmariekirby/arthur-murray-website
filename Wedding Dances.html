A break from the planning chaos

Your lessons become time that's just the two of you, away from the venue, the guest list, and a hundred small decisions. Most couples tell us it's some of the most fun of the whole engagement.

Whatever your timeline

Couples come to us six months out and six weeks out alike. The earlier you start, the more we can polish, but even a handful of lessons gives you a confident plan. Tell us your wedding date and we'll build around it.

How we build your dance

Never danced before? Neither had most of our couples. We start from a simple, elegant sway and add as much as you like: dips, spins, a few showstopping moments. You don't even need your song picked yet, and we'll help with the father-daughter and mother-son dances too.

Or do something unexpected

The slow, traditional first dance isn't for everyone. More and more couples are choosing an upbeat number, a surprise key change, or a bit of fun choreography no one sees coming. If you've got a bold idea, bring it. Those are some of our favorite dances to put together.
